Poaching Detection Infrastructure

A motion-gated YOLOv11 detection pipeline deployed on a Raspberry Pi 3B, triggering inference on sensor events rather than continuous capture to stay inside edge compute limits. Motion-sensor triggers combine with clustering-based spatio-temporal filtering to cut false alerts, and every positive detection automates a 20-second alert clip with AWS EC2 backing for offload and storage.

02 RL — Jan 2026 → Present

Adaptive Traffic Signal Control

A DQN policy trained for adaptive signal control in SUMO, cutting average queue length by 50% against a fixed-time baseline and improving cumulative penalty from −100,000 to −40,000 across evaluation runs. Queue length, waiting time and traffic-state transitions are modelled into a 117-state observation space driving multi-intersection signal timing. Now being extended into a research paper on RL-based traffic signal optimisation.
